# Seasons and car names

A season groups work associated with one annual car cycle. It may include work spanning calendar years; a message's timestamp alone does not establish which car it concerns.

Use `gr26` and `gr27` as season identifiers and GR26 and GR27 as their display names. The numeric suffix distinguishes the car cycle; exact start/end dates and competition-year mapping have not yet been confirmed in this wiki.

- [GR26](gr26/index.md)
- [GR27](gr27/index.md)

When evidence explicitly identifies a car, use that car. If it does not, preserve the uncertainty rather than assigning the current season automatically. Record cross-season comparisons explicitly and retain older designs and decisions as history.

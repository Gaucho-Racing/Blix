# Gaucho Racing

Gaucho Racing builds a new car each year. Designs and decisions can carry forward or change substantially between cars.

Keep knowledge that applies across years here. Put car-specific specifications, decisions, tests, and results under the appropriate [season](../seasons/index.md).

## Evidence

The annual car cycle and need to distinguish GR26 from GR27 were supplied by the team during Blix's knowledge-system planning. No engineering specifications have been imported into this initial page.
